## Chirality

**Handedness**: Protons can be Left (L) or Right (R) handed depending on winding direction:

```
Right-handed (R):        Left-handed (L):

  ╱  (Clockwise)         (CCW)  ╲
```

**This matters for nuclear binding** - L-R pairs bind strongly, L-L or R-R pairs are Pauli suppressed.

In isolated hydrogen, chirality is not observable (no binding partner). It becomes critical in deuterium and heavier nuclei.
