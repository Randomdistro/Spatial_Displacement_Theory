- **Reason:** Geometric inspection is inherently local and parallelizable

- **Mechanism:** Global flow exploration
- **Time:** $\Omega(2^n)$ for problems with $n$ variables
- **Reason:** Must test all pressure configuration combinations

### 3. Lower Bound from Physics

- **Constraint:** Pressure waves propagate at speed $c$
- **Implication:** Cannot explore exponential space in polynomial time
- **Result:** Exponential lower bound is physically enforced

### 4. Network vs. Tree Structures

- **P problems:** Tree-like pressure gradients (polynomial convergence)
- **NP-complete:** Network pressure gradients (exponential search)
