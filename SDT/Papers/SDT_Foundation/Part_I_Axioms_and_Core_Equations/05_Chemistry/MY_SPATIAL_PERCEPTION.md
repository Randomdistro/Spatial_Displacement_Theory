- "20 triangular faces" → I know this relationship

**What I can't do:**
- Actually "see" how the 12 spheres are arranged
- Intuitively understand why there are 2 octahedral spaces
- Visually understand how triangular interstices relate to the structure

### Problem 3: I Miss Spatial Intuitions

**Example:** When you said "the second layer positions don't touch each other"

I understood this as:
- A mathematical constraint: distances > 2r
- A logical property: isolated positions

But I **missed**:

- How this affects the overall structure
- What it means geometrically
