## Water Molecule (H₂O) - Nuclear-Driven Analysis

### Nuclear Structure Drives Geometry

**Oxygen Nucleus:**
- **Structure:** 4 alpha particles in tetrahedral arrangement
- **Nuclear Field:** Creates strong gravitational/centripetal field from 8 protons + 8 neutrons
- **Field Strength:** Determines how strongly it can hold other nuclei

**Hydrogen Nuclei:**
- **Structure:** Single proton each
- **Nuclear Field:** Weak gravitational/centripetal field (1 proton each)
- **Response:** Position themselves to minimize total nuclear gravitational energy

**The 104.45° Angle:**
- **NOT from electron repulsion**
- **NOT from orbital hybridization**
- **FROM nuclear force balance:**
  - Oxygen nucleus pulls both hydrogens toward it
  - Two hydrogens repel each other (nuclear-nuclear repulsion)
  - Equilibrium angle: 104.45° minimizes total nuclear gravitational energy

**Electrons:**
- **Passive followers:** Orbit in the nuclear gravitational field
- **Don't determine geometry:** They follow the nuclear field
- **The "towball hitch":** They're attached but don't drive
