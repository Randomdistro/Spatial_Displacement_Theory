# SDT Solution Plan: The Hodge Conjecture

## Problem Statement

**Millennium Problem:** On a projective non-singular algebraic variety over the complex numbers, any Hodge class is a rational linear combination of classes of algebraic cycles.

In simpler terms: Can we describe certain geometric structures (Hodge classes) in terms of simpler geometric building blocks (algebraic cycles)?
