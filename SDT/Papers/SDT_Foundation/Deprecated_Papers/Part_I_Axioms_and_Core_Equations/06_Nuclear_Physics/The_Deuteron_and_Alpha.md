## 1. The Deuteron ($^2H$)
The Deuteron is the simplest nucleus, composed of one Proton and one Neutron.
In SDT, this is a **Geometric Assembly** of two Shuriken Tori.

### 1.1 The Components
1.  **Proton:** An empty Relativistic Torus ($6\pi$ winding).
2.  **Neutron:** A Relativistic Torus with a **Nestled Electron** in the central well.

### 1.2 The Geometry: Co-Axial Stack
The two Tori stack on top of each other, sharing a common central axis.
*   **Alignment:** Their spins are parallel (Spin 1).
*   **The Bond:** The **Nestled Electron** (from the Neutron) is now shared between the two potential wells. It oscillates between the two centers, acting as a **Nuclear Covalent Bond**.
*   **Mechanism:** This is the physical reality of the "Pion Exchange" model. The electron *is* the exchange particle, binding the two massive tori together.

## 2. The Alpha Particle ($^4He$)
The Alpha Particle is the "Diamond" of nuclear physics: incredibly stable and tightly bound.
In SDT, it is a **Tetrahedral Assembly**.

### 2.1 The Components
*   2 Protons (Empty Tori).
*   2 Neutrons (Tori with Nestled Electrons).
*   **Total:** 4 Tori, 2 Binding Electrons.

### 2.2 The Geometry: The Tetrahedron
The four Tori arrange themselves in a Tetrahedron (the most compact 3D shape).
*   **Faces:** The flat faces of the Tori press against each other.
*   **The Glue:** The **2 Nestled Electrons** migrate to the geometric center of the tetrahedron.
*   **The Lock:** These 2 electrons pull all 4 protons (the 2 original protons + the 2 proton-shells of the neutrons) inwards.
*   **Result:** A magnetically locked, geometrically perfect structure.

### 2.3 Why it is Magic
The Alpha Particle is so stable because it achieves **Geometric Closure**.
*   The 4 Shuriken symmetries interlock.
*   The 2 electrons provide exactly enough negative charge to screen the repulsion and bind the core, without being ejected.
*   It is the first "Complete Shell" of the nuclear lattice.
