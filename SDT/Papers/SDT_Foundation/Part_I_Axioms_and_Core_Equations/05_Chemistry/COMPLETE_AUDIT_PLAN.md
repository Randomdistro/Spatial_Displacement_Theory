## Issues Found and Fixed

### 1. Atomic Radius Derivation
- **Issue:** Force balance gave $r \propto A^{1/3}$, but observations show $r \propto A^{-1/3}$
- **Fix:** Derived from orbital radius formula $r = a_0/Z_{\text{eff}}$ with $Z_{\text{eff}} \propto A^{1/3}$ from field strength per unit area
- **Status:** ✅ FIXED

### 2. Ionization Energy Derivation
- **Issue:** Integration gave inconsistent scaling
- **Fix:** Derived from $I_1 = \int F dr$ with $F \propto R_N^2 Z/r^2$, giving $I_1 \propto A^{5/3} \propto A/r_{\text{atomic}}^2$
- **Status:** ✅ FIXED
