#### 2.4 Movement Budget Analysis

**A. Toroidal Freedom Assessment:**

**Pre-Bonding State (O + 2H, separated):**

**Oxygen Atom:**
- Rotational freedom: Three independent axes
- E_rot,free,O = (1/2)I_O × (ω_x² + ω_y² + ω_z²)
- I_O ≈ 1.3 × 10⁻⁴⁶ kg·m² (moment of inertia)
- Available rotational energy: E_rot,free,O ≈ k_B × T (thermal, ~0.025 eV at 300 K)

**Each Hydrogen Atom:**
- Rotational freedom: Three independent axes
- E_rot,free,H = (1/2)I_H × (ω_x² + ω_y² + ω_z²)
- I_H ≈ 1.67 × 10⁻⁴⁷ kg·m²
- Available rotational energy: E_rot,free,H ≈ k_B × T

**Total Pre-Bonding Rotational Energy:**
- E_rot,free,total = E_rot,free,O + 2 × E_rot,free,H ≈ 0.075 eV (at 300 K)

**Post-Bonding State (H₂O molecule):**

**Constrained Axes (NUCLEUS-DRIVEN):**
- O-H nuclear force connections: **Highly constrained** (~90% constraint)
  - The nuclear gravitational forces fix the positions of H nuclei relative to O nucleus
  - Nuclear force angles are nearly rigid (small amplitude vibrations only)
  - Electrons follow the nuclear field - their motion is constrained by nuclear forces
- Molecular rotation: **Partially constrained** (~40% constraint)
  - Three rotational axes remain (molecular tumbling)
  - But moment of inertia is larger, reducing rotational energy
  - Nuclear forces maintain the bent geometry during rotation
- Internal rotation: **Fully constrained** (rigid structure)
  - No free rotation about nuclear force connections (bent structure is rigid)
  - Nuclear forces determine the geometry - electrons follow

**Remaining Rotational Energy:**
- E_rot,molecular = (1/2)I_H2O × (ω_a² + ω_b² + ω_c²)
- I_H2O ≈ 1.0 × 10⁻⁴⁷ kg·m² (average moment of inertia)
- Rotational constants: 
  - A = 27.9 cm⁻¹ (rotation about a-axis)
  - B = 14.5 cm⁻¹ (rotation about b-axis)
  - C = 9.3 cm⁻¹ (rotation about c-axis)
- Molecular rotation: ω_mol ≈ 1.0 × 10¹² rad/s (average)
- E_rot,bonded ≈ 0.030 eV (at 300 K)

**Vibrational Modes Created:**
- Three vibrational modes:
  1. **Symmetric stretch (ν₁):** 3657 cm⁻¹ = 1.10 × 10¹⁴ Hz
     - E_vib,1 = (1/2)hν₁ = 0.227 eV (zero-point energy)
  2. **Bending (ν₂):** 1595 cm⁻¹ = 4.78 × 10¹³ Hz
     - E_vib,2 = (1/2)hν₂ = 0.099 eV (zero-point energy)
  3. **Asymmetric stretch (ν₃):** 3756 cm⁻¹ = 1.13 × 10¹⁴ Hz
     - E_vib,3 = (1/2)hν₃ = 0.233 eV (zero-point energy)
- Total vibrational energy (zero-point): E_vib,total = 0.559 eV

**Energy Redistribution:**
- ΔE_rot = E_rot,free,total - E_rot,bonded ≈ 0.045 eV
- This energy is transferred to vibrational modes
- Additional energy from nuclear force formation: E_nuclear = 4.84 eV per bond = 9.68 eV total
- Net energy change: ΔE_total = -9.68 eV (energy released when nuclei form stable nuclear force configuration)
- **This is nuclear gravitational energy, not electron energy**

**Interrotation Delay:**
- τ = I_eff / (k_constraint × E_constraint)
- I_eff ≈ 1.0 × 10⁻⁴⁷ kg·m²
- k_constraint ≈ 5 × 10²⁶ N/m (from bond stiffness)
- E_constraint ≈ 0.045 eV = 7.2 × 10⁻²¹ J
- τ ≈ 3 × 10⁻¹² s (femtosecond timescale)

**Overpowering Threshold (Nuclear Force):**
- E_constraint,max = E_nuclear / 2 = 4.84 eV / 2 = 2.42 eV per nuclear force connection
- Current constraint: ~0.045 eV << 2.42 eV
- **Status:** Stable - nuclear forces maintain geometry, energy successfully redistributed, no overpowering
- **The nuclear forces are strong enough to maintain the bent geometry against thermal motion**

**B. Ionization Thresholds:**

**First Ionization Energy:**
- **Value:** 12.62 eV (H₂O → H₂O⁺ + e⁻)

- **Ion Stability:** H₂O⁺ is reactive, seeks electron to restore serenity

**Second Ionization Energy:**
- **Value:** 18.5 eV (H₂O⁺ → H₂O²⁺ + e⁻)
- **Mechanism:** Removing second electron breaks O-H bonds
- **Ion Stability:** H₂O²⁺ is highly unstable, dissociates immediately

**Proton Affinity:**
- **Value:** 691 kJ/mol = 7.16 eV (H₂O → H₃O⁺)
- **Mechanism:** H₂O accepts H⁺ to form H₃O⁺
- **Ion Stability:** H₃O⁺ is stable in aqueous solution

**C. Bond Breaking Energies:**

**O-H Dissociation:**
- **Dissociation Energy (D₀):** 493 kJ/mol = 5.11 eV
- **Activation Energy:** Variable (depends on pathway)
- **Reaction Pathways:**
  - **Homolytic:** H₂O → OH + H (requires 5.11 eV)
  - **Heterolytic:** H₂O → H⁺ + OH⁻ (requires 7.16 eV, but stabilized in solution)

**SDT Mechanism (NUCLEUS-DRIVEN):** Breaking an O-H bond requires overcoming the nuclear gravitational/centripetal force that holds the hydrogen nucleus in orbit around the oxygen nucleus. The energy required (5.11 eV) equals the depth of the nuclear gravitational well. The electron is passively following - breaking the bond means overcoming the nuclear force, not electron-electron interactions.
