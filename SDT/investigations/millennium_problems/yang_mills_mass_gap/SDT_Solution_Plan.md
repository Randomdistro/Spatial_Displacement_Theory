# SDT Solution Plan: Yang–Mills Existence and Mass Gap

## Problem Statement

**Millennium Problem:** Prove that for any compact simple gauge group $G$, a non-trivial quantum Yang–Mills theory exists on $\mathbb{R}^4$ and has a mass gap $\Delta > 0$.

The mass gap means the lightest particle has non-zero mass - there's a gap between the vacuum (zero energy) and the first excited state.

**Core Insight:** Yang–Mills theory describes **gauge fields** which in SDT are **pressure field configurations**. The mass gap corresponds to the **minimum energy** required to create a stable turbine cell from pure spation.

**SDT Interpretation:** You cannot create matter (turbine cells) with zero energy. There's a **minimum energy threshold** set by the master equation.

The master equation $\dot{E} = P_\infty A_{\text{eff}} \Gamma \kappa (1-\eta)$ provides the mass gap:

**For vacuum (no matter):**
- $A_{\text{eff}} = 0$ (no turbine cells)

**For particle (turbine cell):**

**Mass Gap:**
$$\Delta = E_{\text{particle}} - E_{\text{vacuum}} = P_\infty A_{\text{eff}} \Gamma \kappa (1-\eta) \times \tau > 0$$

This is **strictly positive** because:
