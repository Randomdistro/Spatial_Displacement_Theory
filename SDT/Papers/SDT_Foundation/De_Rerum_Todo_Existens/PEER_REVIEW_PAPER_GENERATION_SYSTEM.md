This system generates **individual, standalone peer-review papers** from SDT content. Each paper covers **ONE specific aspect** of SDT, written to the highest academic standards for submission to physics, chemistry, or interdisciplinary journals.

**Key Principle:** Each paper must be:
- **Self-contained** (readable without other SDT papers)
- **Focused** (one specific aspect, not a general overview)
- **Rigorous** (complete proofs, no handwaving)
- **Validated** (quantitative comparison with experiment)
- **Publishable** (meets journal standards)
