## PART I: VOLUME SCOPE AND ORGANIZATION

### 1.1 Volume Theme

Volume 4 focuses on **inorganic and mineral molecules** - compounds found in Earth's crust, minerals, rocks, and geological formations. These include oxides, sulfides, halides, carbonates, silicates, and other inorganic compounds that form the solid foundation of our planet.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Typically ionic or covalent inorganic compounds
- Found in minerals and rocks
- Form crystal structures (nuclear force lattices)
- High melting points (strong nuclear forces)
- Often contain metals (transition metals, alkali metals, alkaline earth metals)

### 1.2 Classification Criteria

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Inorganic compounds (no C-C bonds, except carbonates)
- Found in minerals or geological formations
- Stable crystal structures
- Well-characterized experimentally
- Common in Earth's crust

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Organic compounds (covered in Volumes 2-3)
- Highly synthetic inorganic compounds
- Unstable or theoretical compounds
