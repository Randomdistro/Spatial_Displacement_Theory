## PART I: VOLUME SCOPE AND ORGANIZATION

### 1.1 Volume Theme

Volume 6 focuses on **nitrogen compounds** - molecules containing nitrogen in various oxidation states and bonding configurations. These represent the nitrogen cycle in nature: from atmospheric N₂ through fixation to nitrates, and including biological nitrogen compounds. The volume covers nitrogen's central role in life (amino acids, nucleic acids), agriculture (fertilizers), industry (explosives, materials), and environmental processes (atmospheric chemistry).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Nitrogen compounds in all oxidation states (-3 to +5)
- Diatomic N₂ (atmospheric nitrogen, triple bond)
- Ammonia and ammonium compounds
- Nitrogen oxides (NO, NO₂, N₂O, etc.)
- Nitrates and nitrites
- Hydrazine and derivatives
- Azides and nitrogen-halogen compounds
- Biological nitrogen compounds
- Industrial nitrogen compounds

### 1.2 Classification Criteria

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Contains nitrogen atoms
- Stable or metastable compounds
- Natural occurrence or industrial importance
- Well-characterized experimentally
- Includes ions, radicals, and complexes
- Covers all nitrogen oxidation states

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Complex biological polymers with nitrogen (covered in Volume 3)
- Organometallic nitrogen complexes (reserved for later volumes)
- Highly unstable theoretical compounds

**CRITICAL:** The N≡N triple bond is NOT three electron pairs - it is three nuclear force connections.

- Each connection: Nuclear force well of specific depth
- Total bond energy: Sum of three nuclear force well depths
- NOT π-bonding - nuclear force network

**B. Square Pyramid Completion:**
- Each N atom: 5 valence electrons (Square Pyramid geometry)
- Triple bond: Completes both cubes (5 + 3 = 8)
- Nuclear field symmetry creates stable structure
- Track: Bond length from nuclear force equilibrium

**C. Bond Energy Breakdown:**
- Energy per nuclear connection: ~315 kJ/mol
- Total: ~946 kJ/mol for three connections
- NOT resonance energy - nuclear force summation
- Calculate from nuclear field strengths (14:14 interaction)
