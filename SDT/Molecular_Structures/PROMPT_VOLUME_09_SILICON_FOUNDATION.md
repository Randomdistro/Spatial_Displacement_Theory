## PART I: VOLUME SCOPE AND ORGANIZATION

### 1.1 Volume Theme

Volume 9 focuses on **silicon compounds** - molecules containing silicon in various bonding configurations. Silicon is the second most abundant element in Earth's crust and forms the foundation of our planet's solid structure. The volume covers silicon dioxide (quartz, cristobalite), silanes, silicon carbide, silicates (orthosilicates, chain, sheet, framework), silicon halides, organosilicons, and silicones. These compounds are essential in geology, materials science, electronics, and industry.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Silicon in various oxidation states (-4 to +4)
- Silicon dioxide polymorphs (quartz, cristobalite, tridymite, stishovite)
- Silanes (SiH₄, Si₂H₆, etc.)
- Silicon carbide (SiC)
- Silicates (orthosilicates, pyrosilicates, chain, sheet, framework)
- Silicon halides
- Organosilicons and silicones
- Semiconductor properties from nuclear field structure

### 1.2 Classification Criteria

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Contains silicon atoms
- Stable or metastable compounds
- Natural occurrence or industrial importance
- Well-characterized experimentally
- Includes ions, radicals, and complexes
- Covers all silicon bonding patterns

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Complex organosilicon polymers (reserved for later volumes)
