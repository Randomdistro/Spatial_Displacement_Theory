## PART I: VOLUME SCOPE AND ORGANIZATION

### 1.1 Volume Theme

Volume 2 focuses on **simple organic molecules** - molecules containing carbon-carbon bonds and carbon-hydrogen bonds, typically with fewer than 10 carbon atoms. These represent the fundamental building blocks of organic chemistry, formed through natural processes (biological, geological) and simple synthetic pathways.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Carbon-carbon single, double, and triple bonds
- Carbon-hydrogen bonds
- Simple functional groups (OH, NH₂, COOH, etc.)
- Linear, branched, and cyclic structures
- Natural occurrence or simple synthesis

### 1.2 Classification Criteria

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Contains carbon atoms
- Typically 1-10 carbon atoms
- May contain H, O, N, S, P, halogens
- Formed through natural or simple synthetic processes
- Stable under standard conditions
- Well-characterized experimentally

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Complex polymers (>10 repeating units)
- Highly synthetic compounds requiring extreme conditions
- Unstable radicals (unless specifically included)
- Organometallic complexes (reserved for later volumes)
