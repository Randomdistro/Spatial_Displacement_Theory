## Word-count strategy (from original brief)

- **2680 words ≈ 4–5 pages** (12pt, double-spaced). **6064 words ≈ 10–11 pages.**  
- Open with **200–400 words** of context and signposting.  
- **40–50%** main exposition (formulas, derivation, variables).  
- **30–40%** commentary (“why it matters,” physical meaning, scale invariance).  
- **10–20%** checks, cross-references, worked numbers or tables.  
- To reach minimum: add step-by-step derivations, repeat formula in symbolic and verbal form, 2–3 short commentary paragraphs per major formula.  
- To stay under maximum: keep long tables compact; move exhaustive cross-reference lists to collapsible or end sections if the format allows.
