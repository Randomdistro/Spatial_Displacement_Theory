## The Proton

**Mass**: 938.27 MeV/c² (1.673 × 10⁻²⁷ kg)  
**Radius**: R_p = 0.84 fm  
**Density**: ρ_p = 1836 × ρ_e (1836 times denser than electron)

- Major radius: 0.84 fm
- Minor radius: ~0.28 fm (R_p/3)
- Winding number: 3 (trefoil knot)

**Not point-like, not composite** - it's a **topologically stable compression** of spation.

### CMB Pressure Containment

The proton is compressed by **external CMB pressure**:
- P_CMB ≈ 1.1 × 10²² Pa (acting on surface)
- Internal spation density: ρ_s = 2.27 × 10¹⁷ kg/m³
- Balance: Internal pressure = External pressure

**Mass emerges from compression**:
$$m_p c^2 = \rho_s c^2 V_{torus}$$

where V = 2π²R_p(R_p/3)² ≈ 0.41 fm³

### Stability

**Lifetime**: >10³⁴ years

**Why stable?** Trefoil knot cannot unwind without breaking spation continuity. It's the **ground state** displacement configuration.

## The Electron

**Mass**: 0.511 MeV/c² (9.109 × 10⁻³¹ kg)  
**Radius**: r_e ≈ 10⁻²² m (Compton scale)  
**Density**: ρ_e = ρ_p / 1836

### Structure

**NOT a point particle** - it's a **compressed spation vortex** at much lower density than proton.

**CMB Pressure**:
- P_CMB acts on electron surface
- Much lower compression than proton (1/1836)
- Creates mass m_e = 0.511 MeV/c²

### In Atoms

**Held by electrostatic force** (centripetal):
$$F = \frac{e^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0 r^2}$$

This is **NOT balanced by centrifugal** - electron is TRAPPED in spation pressure well.

**To ionize**: Must inject kinetic energy E ≥ 13.6 eV to overcome electrostatic binding.

**Orbital velocity**: v = cα√(R_Bohr/R_p) ≈ 1.84c (from balance equations, NOT from centrifugal force)

### Why 1.84c?

Balance of:
1. **Electrostatic pull** (inward from proton)
2. **Occlusion pressure gradient** (proton's displacement creates pressure well)
3. **CMB pressure** (external, 1.1×10²² Pa on lepton)

At radius a₀ = 52,917 fm, these lock into resonance.

## The Neutrino

**Mass**: < 0.1 eV/c² (nearly massless)  
**Cross-section**: σ_ν ≈ 10⁻⁴⁴ cm² (extremely small)  
**Mean free path in matter**: Light-years through lead!

### Evidence for Reality

1. **Beta decay**: n → p + e⁻ + ν̄_e (observed, measured)
2. **Solar neutrinos**: 65 billion/cm²/s at Earth (detected)
3. **Supernova 1987A**: Neutrino burst detected 3 hours before light
4. **Nuclear reactors**: Emit ~10²⁰ neutrinos/s (detected nearby)

**These are REAL particles**, not mathematical artifacts.

### Role in SDT

**Neutrinos are the "grease"** - they mediate nuclear binding:

When neutron's internal electron transitions between nucleons, it emits neutrino to carry phase. In stable nuclei, these neutrinos **circulate** (not escape), creating binding energy.

**Binding energy** = N_ν × E_ν

where:
- N_ν = neutrino mode count (from geometry)
- E_ν ≈ 1.57 MeV (from alpha particle)

### Neutrino Cross-Section

**Measured**: σ_ν ~ 10⁻⁴⁴ cm² for MeV neutrinos

**SDT interpretation**: Neutrinos interact ONLY when phase-matched to nuclear geometry. Most pass through because they're not resonant with trefoil circulation.

**In nuclei**: Resonant neutrinos are TRAPPED - their "cross-section" is effectively infinite (permanent capture in circulation mode).

## The Neutron (Composite)

**Mass**: 939.57 MeV/c² (slightly heavier than proton)  
**Composition**: Proton + Internal Electron  
**Density**: ρ_n = 1838 × ρ_e

### Structure

**NOT a fundamental particle** - it's:
- Proton trefoil (6π winding)
- Plus electron nestled in donut hole
- Electron compressed to ~10⁻²² m

**This explains**:
- Slightly higher mass (mp + me = 938.78 MeV ≈ mn)
- Opposite magnetic moment (electron opposes proton field)
- Instability (electron can escape → β⁻ decay)

### Decay

**Free neutron lifetime**: 879.4 seconds

$$n \to p + e^- + \bar{\nu}_e$$

**SDT**: Internal electron unwinds from trefoil, escapes. Antineutrino ("grease") ejected to maintain phase balance.

**In nuclei**: Electron stabilized by neighboring proton - forms binding bridge. Neutron stable.
