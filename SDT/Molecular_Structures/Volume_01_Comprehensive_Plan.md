## Comprehensive Implementation Plan (~400 Molecules)

**Theme:** The First Molecules in the Universe  
**Focus:** Stellar Nucleosynthesis Products  
**Classification:** Most Natural (Scale 1)  
**Date:** December 2025

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Volume 1 catalogs approximately 400 molecules that form in stellar nucleosynthesis, interstellar clouds, and early planetary formation. These represent the most natural, primordial molecular systems - those that form spontaneously under cosmic conditions without human intervention.

**Organization Principle:** Molecules are grouped by structural complexity and bonding patterns, progressing from simplest (diatomic) to more complex (triatomic, tetraatomic, etc.), always prioritizing natural formation pathways.
