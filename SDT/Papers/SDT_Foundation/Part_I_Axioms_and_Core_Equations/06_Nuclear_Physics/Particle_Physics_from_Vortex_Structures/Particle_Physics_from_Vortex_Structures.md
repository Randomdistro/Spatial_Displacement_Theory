$$V_q = 3 \times V_{\text{component}} = 3 \times \frac{4\pi}{3} r_q^3 = 4\pi r_q^3 \tag{2.1}$$

where $r_q \sim 0.1$ fm is the characteristic quark radius.

**From nucleon structure:** The total displacement volume per nucleon is $V_n = 2.76 \times 10^{-45}$ m³ (see Gravitation from Spation Pressure Gradients, §2.2). For three quarks per nucleon:
$$V_q = \frac{V_n}{3} = 9.2 \times 10^{-46} \text{ m}^3 \tag{2.1a}$$

**Physical meaning:** Color is not a fundamental charge but a **geometric property** of how the quark vortex orients itself in the spation pressure field. The three-fold structure allows three distinct orientations, giving three color states.

**Step 3: Flavor Hierarchy from Circulation Scale**
