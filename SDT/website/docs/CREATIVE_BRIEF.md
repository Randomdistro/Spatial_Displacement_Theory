## The Flower of Life: Landing Experience

### Visual Concept

**The Pattern:**
- Sacred geometry Flower of Life
- 7-19 interleaved torus rings
- Arranged in hexagonal flower pattern
- Not flat—3D, volumetric, breathing

**The Material:**
- Deep Space Blue base (#1a365d)
- Subtle metallic sheen (0.8 metallic, 0.2 roughness)
- Gentle emissive glow (0.1 intensity)
- Gold gradient on active selection

**The Motion:**
- Slow, organic rotation (0.5 RPM)
- Each ring slightly different (0.4-0.6 RPM)
- Creates mesmerizing flow
- Suggests the spation medium itself

**The Interaction:**
- Hover: Gentle scale (1.1x), increased glow
- Selection: Flip and reveal (180° rotation)
- Transition: Smooth, flowing, never jarring
- Camera: Floats through space

**The Feeling:**
- Contemplative
- Mysterious but inviting
- Scientific but sacred
- Complex but simple

## Spatial Composition

### Camera Philosophy
- Smooth, orbital motion
- Never jarring
- Always flowing
- Like floating through space

### Node Rooms
- Each node is a "chamber"
- Subtle boundaries (zones, not walls)
- Content floats in space
- Depth creates hierarchy

### Lighting
- Soft, volumetric
- No harsh shadows
- Ambient glow
- Everything feels alive

---

## Animation Language

### Principles
1. **Everything flows** - No sudden stops
2. **Respect physics** - Ease in/out, momentum
3. **Subtle motion** - Draw attention, don't distract
4. **Purposeful** - Every animation has meaning

### Timing
- Fast (150ms): Micro-interactions
- Medium (300ms): Panel transitions
- Slow (1000ms+): Camera movements

### Easing
- Default: Smooth, natural
- Organic: Slight bounce, playful
- Never linear (feels robotic)

## The Obviousness Test

Before finalizing any design:

1. **Does it feel inevitable?** (Not forced)
2. **Does geometry sing?** (Not just functional)
3. **Is it subtle?** (Not garish)
4. **Does it reveal?** (Not obscure)
5. **Is it visceral?** (Can you feel it?)

If all yes → It's right.

## Key Design Decisions

### Why Flower of Life?
- Sacred geometry = universal structure
- Interleaved rings = interconnected concepts
- 3D pattern = spatial navigation
- Beautiful = draws people in

### Why Deep Blue + Gold?
- Blue = space, depth, foundation
- Gold = energy, revelation, flow
- Complementary but not clashing
- Scientific but not sterile

### Why Slow Animations?
- Contemplative experience
- Respects the theory's depth
- Allows understanding to sink in
- Not rushed, not flashy

### Why 3D Spatial Navigation?
- SDT is about space
- Navigation = understanding
- Spatial = conceptual
- The medium is the message
