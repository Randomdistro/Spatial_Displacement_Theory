### Binding Energy Calculation

Total binding energy for N-atom molecule:
E_bind = Σᵢⱼ E_bond(i,j) - E_isolated

Where E_isolated = Σᵢ E_atom(i)

**For benzene (C₆H₆):**
6 C-C bonds, 6 C-H bonds
E_bond(C-C) = 3.47×10⁻¹⁹ J (from Phase 2)
E_bond(C-H) = 4.13×10⁻¹⁹ J

E_bind(benzene) = 6 × 3.47×10⁻¹⁹ + 6 × 4.13×10⁻¹⁹ - (6 × E_C + 6 × E_H)

### Thermodynamic Stability

Gibbs free energy from SDT:
ΔG = Ė × τ_react - T × ΔS

Where τ_react = reaction time = r_react / (c × α)

**For H₂ + ½O₂ → H₂O:**
r_react = 2.5×10⁻¹⁰ m (transition state radius)
τ_react = 2.5×10⁻¹⁰ / (2.99792458×10⁸ × 7.2973525693×10⁻³) = 2.5×10⁻¹⁰ / 2.187×10⁶ = 1.143×10⁻¹⁶ s

ΔĖ = Ė(H₂O) - Ė(H₂) - ½Ė(O₂) = 2.574×10⁵² - 1.763×10⁵² - 0.728×10⁵² = 8.3×10⁵¹ J·s⁻¹

ΔG = 9.487×10³⁵ - 298.15 × (-163.2) = 9.487×10³⁵ + 4.867×10⁴ J·mol⁻¹
