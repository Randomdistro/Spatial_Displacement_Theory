## EXOTIC EXAMPLE 1: Sulfate Ion (SO₄²⁻) - Tetrahedral Oxoanion

**Formula:** SO₄²⁻  
**Atoms:** 5 (1 S + 4 O)  
**Charge:** -2  
**Geometry:** Tetrahedral (T_d point group)

### SDT Nuclear Structure Analysis

**Sulfur Atom (S, Z=16):**
- **Nuclear Structure:** 8 alpha particles (octahedral/tetrahedral arrangement)
- **Composition:** 16 protons + 16 neutrons = 32 nucleons
- **Nuclear Field Strength:** 32x (32 nucleons)
- **Building Blocks:** 8α

**Four Oxygen Atoms (O, Z=8, ×4):**
- **Reference:** See Volume 7 Section 2.1 for baseline O-16 structure
- **Nuclear Field Strength:** 16x each (16 nucleons, 4 alpha particles each)

### SDT Predictions

| Property | SDT Prediction | Nuclear Interpretation |
|----------|----------------|----------------------|
| **S-O Bond Length** | ~149.0 pm | Nuclear force equilibrium distance (32:16 ratio, single bond) |
| **S-O Bond Energy** | ~523 kJ/mol = 5.42 eV | Depth of nuclear gravitational well (32:16 interaction) |
| **O-S-O Bond Angle** | 109.47° (perfect tetrahedral) | Nuclear force minimization - four O nuclei at tetrahedral vertices |
| **Geometry** | Tetrahedral (T_d) | Nuclear force balance creates perfect tetrahedron |
| **First Ionization Energy** | ~5.0 eV | Removing electron from O nuclear field (16 nucleons), modified by molecular environment |
| **ν(S-O stretch) (cm⁻¹)** | ~983 | Nuclear force connection vibration (32:16 ratio) |

**Nuclear Explanation:**
- Sulfur nucleus (32 nucleons, 8 alpha particles) creates nuclear field
- Four oxygen nuclei (16 nucleons each, 4 alpha particles each) position at tetrahedral vertices
- Perfect tetrahedral geometry from nuclear force balance (NOT electron repulsion)
- -2 charge affects nuclear field distribution
