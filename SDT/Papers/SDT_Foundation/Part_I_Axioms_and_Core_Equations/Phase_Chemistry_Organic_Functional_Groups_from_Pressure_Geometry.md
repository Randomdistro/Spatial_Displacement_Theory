# Phase Chemistry: Organic Functional Groups from Pressure Geometry

## Abstract

This phase derives organic functional groups (alcohols, ethers, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, amines) from Spatial Displacement Theory (SDT) using specific pressure field geometries. Each functional group has characteristic pressure field patterns from heteroatom occlusion. Reactivity derives from pressure field differences. All from pressure field mechanics.

## 2. Alcohols and Ethers

### 2.1 Alcohols (R-OH)

- O-H bond: Polar (O more electronegative)
- Pressure field asymmetry → polarity
- Can donate H⁺ (weak acid)

### 2.2 Ethers (R-O-R)

- C-O-C linkage
- No O-H → less reactive
- Good solvents

---

## 3. Carbonyl Compounds

### 3.1 Aldehydes (R-CHO)

- C=O bond: Polar
- Electrophilic carbon
- Pressure field asymmetry

### 3.2 Ketones (R-COR')

- Similar to aldehydes
- Less reactive (steric + electronic)

### 3.3 Carboxylic Acids (R-COOH)

- COOH group: Can donate H⁺
- Resonance stabilization
- pKa ~4-5

---

## 4. Amines

### 4.1 Amines (R-NH₂, R₂NH, R₃N)

- N-H bonds: Can accept H⁺
- Basic (pKb ~3-4)
- Pressure field stabilization of NH₄⁺
