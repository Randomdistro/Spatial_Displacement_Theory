This high geometric perfection makes Nickel the "Stainless" surface of the world, coating everything from coins to steel in a layer of inert, self-satisfied atoms.

### On the 14-Alpha Lattice: Nickel-58
Nickel-58 (68% abundance) is the major isotope.
-   **Structure:** 28 protons, 30 neutrons.
-   **SDT Model:** **14 Alpha Particles + 2 Neutrons**.
    -   $14 \times 2 = 28$ Protons.
    -   $14 \times 2 = 28$ Neutrons + 2 = 30.
    -   **Deuteron Locking:** Why 2 extra neutrons? In SDT, these are not free-floating; they are part of two "Triton" blocks ($^3H$) incorporated into the 12-Alpha lattice, or perhaps more accurately, the lattice consists of 12 Alphas + 2 Tritons.

### On the Waning Magnetism
Why is Nickel the weakest ferromagnet?
-   **Config:** $3d^8$.
-   **The Geometry:** We have 3 Paired Dyads and 2 Unpaired Vortices.
-   **The Physics:** The 3 dyads are internally magnetic-neutral (wakes cancel). Only 2 electrons are left to "mesh" with neighbors. The mesh is there, but it is weak. The thermal vibration of room temperature (300K) is almost enough to shake them loose (Curie Temp 627K vs 1043K for Fe).
