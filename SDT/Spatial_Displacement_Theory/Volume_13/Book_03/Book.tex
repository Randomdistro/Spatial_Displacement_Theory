\chapter{Legacy Compilations}

\section*{Abstract}
Legacy compilations remain part of the SDT record. This book describes how alternate builds and compiled
artifacts are interpreted and reconciled with the canonical SDT structure.

\section{Compilation Integrity}
Alternate builds are evaluated by consistency with the master equation and with the geometry of occlusion.

\chapter{Migration to Canon}

\section*{Abstract}
Legacy artifacts are migrated into the canonical structure by mapping their formulas to current SDT
notation and verifying dimensional consistency.

\section{Migration Rule}
If a legacy formula can be written in master-equation form, it is retained; otherwise it is archived.

\chapter{Alternate Build Catalog}

\section*{Abstract}
Alternate builds are cataloged to preserve historical context. This catalog is not canonical physics but
is essential for traceability.

\section{Catalog Fields}
Each build records version, differences, validation status, and canonical mapping results.
