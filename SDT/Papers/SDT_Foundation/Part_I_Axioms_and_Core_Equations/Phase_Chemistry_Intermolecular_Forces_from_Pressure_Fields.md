# Phase Chemistry: Intermolecular Forces from Pressure Fields

## Abstract

This phase extends Phase 18 (Van der Waals) and Phase 17 (hydrogen bonding) to provide comprehensive coverage of all intermolecular forces from Spatial Displacement Theory (SDT). All forces derive from pressure field interactions between molecules. See Phase 18 and Phase 17 for detailed derivations.

---

## 1. Physical Foundation

### 1.1 Master Equation for Intermolecular Forces

All intermolecular forces derive from the master equation:

$$\boxed{\dot{E} = P_{\mathrm{CMB}} \cdot A_{\mathrm{eff}} \cdot \Gamma \cdot \kappa \cdot (1-\eta)} \tag{1.1}$$

For intermolecular interactions:
- $A_{\mathrm{eff}}$ = effective occlusion area between molecules
- $\Gamma$ = circulation factor for molecular motion
- $\kappa$ = curvature (inverse intermolecular distance)
- $(1-\eta)$ = coupling strength (varies by force type)

## 2. Force Types

### 2.1 Hydrogen Bonding

See Phase 17 Section 4 for detailed derivation.

**From master equation:**
$$E_{\mathrm{H-bond}} = P_{\mathrm{CMB}} A_{\mathrm{H}} A_{\mathrm{X}} \Gamma \kappa (1-\eta_{\mathrm{H-bond}}) \times \tau \tag{2.1}$$

where X = N, O, F (strong pressure field coupling).

**SDT explanation:** H-bond = special case of pressure field attraction when H bonded to N, O, F.

### 2.2 Dipole-Dipole

See Phase 18 for detailed derivation.

**From master equation:**
$$E_{\mathrm{dipole-dipole}} = P_{\mathrm{CMB}} A_{\mathrm{dipole,1}} A_{\mathrm{dipole,2}} \Gamma \kappa (1-\eta_{\mathrm{dipole}}) \times \tau \tag{2.2}$$

**SDT explanation:** Permanent pressure field asymmetry creates attraction.

### 2.3 London Dispersion

See Phase 18 for detailed derivation.

**From master equation:**
$$E_{\mathrm{London}} = P_{\mathrm{CMB}} A_{\mathrm{fluctuation}} \Gamma \kappa (1-\eta_{\mathrm{fluctuation}}) \times \tau_{\mathrm{fluctuation}} \tag{2.3}$$

**SDT explanation:** Temporary pressure field fluctuations create attraction.

### 2.4 Ion-Dipole

**From master equation:**
$$E_{\mathrm{ion-dipole}} = P_{\mathrm{CMB}} A_{\mathrm{ion}} A_{\mathrm{dipole}} \Gamma \kappa (1-\eta_{\mathrm{ion-dipole}}) \times \tau \tag{2.4}$$

**SDT explanation:** Pressure field gradient between ion and dipole.
