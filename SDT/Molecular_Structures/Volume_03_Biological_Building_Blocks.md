- **Biological Role:** 
  - Simplest amino acid
  - Non-polar, non-chiral
  - Protein building block
  - Neurotransmitter (inhibitory)
  - Collagen component (every third residue)

#### Section 2: Atomic Constituents Analysis (NUCLEUS-DRIVEN)

**A. Carbon Atom 1 (Carboxyl Carbon):**

**Nuclear Structure:**
- **Building Blocks:** 3 alpha particles (triangular arrangement)

- **Nuclear Geometry:** Triangular arrangement of 3α creates nuclear field

**Nuclear Field:**
- Creates nuclear gravitational/centripetal field from 12 nucleons
- Field strength determines electron orbital positions
- Electrons orbit in nuclear field (passive followers)

**Geometric Calculus Equation:**
- From atomica sentis: C-12 = 3α (triangular)
- Nuclear structure drives electron configuration
- 6 electrons orbit in nuclear field of 12 nucleons

**D. Oxygen Atom 1 (Carboxyl Oxygen):**

**Nuclear Field Interactions:**

**C. Molecular Geometry:**

- N adopts pyramidal geometry (107°) due to nuclear force balance with 3 substituents

**D. Hydrogen Bonding:**

**In peptide form:** N-H and C=O groups can form hydrogen bonds (nuclear force networks) with other amino acids.

#### Section 4: Movement Budget Analysis

**A. Toroidal Freedom Assessment:**

**Pre-bonding:**
- Individual atoms have rotational freedom
- C, N, O atoms: High rotational energy
- H atoms: High rotational energy

**Post-bonding:**
- C₁-C₂ bond: Constrains rotation (single bond allows some rotation)
- C₁=O₁ bond: No rotation (double bond, rigid)
- C₁-O₂ bond: Constrains rotation
- C₂-N bond: Constrains rotation
- All H bonds: Constrain rotation

**Energy Redistribution:**
- Rotational energy converted to vibrational modes
- Bond formation releases energy (bond energies)
- System stabilizes at nuclear force equilibrium

**Interrotation Delay:**
- C₁-C₂: ~10⁻¹² s (single bond rotation barrier)
- C₂-N: ~10⁻¹² s (single bond rotation barrier)
- C₁=O₁: No rotation (double bond)

**Overpowering Threshold:**
- C-C bond breaking: 347 kJ/mol = 3.60 eV
- C=O bond breaking: 799 kJ/mol = 8.28 eV
- C-N bond breaking: 305 kJ/mol = 3.16 eV

**B. Quantification:**

**Bond Formation Energies:**
- Total bond energy: ~3500 kJ/mol (sum of all bonds)
- Formation releases energy (exothermic)

**Stability:**
- Stable at room temperature
- All bonds well below overpowering threshold
- Nuclear force equilibrium maintained
