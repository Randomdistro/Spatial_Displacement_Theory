# Nuclear Structure Comprehensive Investigation Probe

## Overview

This investigation builds an **excessively detailed mathematical probe** into nuclear structure that explores:

- **All atomic states**: What atoms ARE, what they COULD BE, what they TRANSFORM INTO, what they FUSE INTO, what they DECAY INTO
- **All charge states**: All ionizations, all cations, all anions
- **All excitations**: All energy levels, all transitions, all spectral lines
- **All dynamics**: All velocities, all speeds, all timings
- **Electron participation**: Complete integration of nuclear structure with atomic electron structuring
