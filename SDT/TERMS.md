## Fundamental Concepts

### 28-Dimensional State Manifold (Ξ ∈ ℝ²⁸)
The complete state vector describing any physical locus in SDT, consisting of 28 aspects organized into 7 hierarchical levels:
- **Level 1 (1)**: Zero-point (ξ₀ - existence)
- **Level 2 (2)**: Line (ξ₁₀ location, ξ₁₁ relocation)
- **Level 3 (3)**: Plane (ξ_p0, ξ_p1, ξ_p2 - boundaries, position, rotation)
- **Level 4 (4)**: Sphere (ξ_s0-3 - volume, motion, rotation, orientation)
- **Level 5 (5)**: Torus (T₁-₅ - ring, diameter, surface, volume, gradation)
- **Level 6 (6)**: Dynamism (Φ₀-₅ - omnidirectionality, translocation, oscillation, chirality, variance, phase transitions)
- **Level 7 (7)**: Energy (ε₀-₅,b - potential, kinetic, rotational, field, binding, flux, transmission)

**Physical meaning**: Natural extension of Euclidean geometry to dynamic toroidal physics. Force hierarchy (10³⁹ Coulomb/Gravity ratio) and pressure hierarchy (10⁻¹²³ vacuum/lattice) emerge from these aspects.

**Choice-space evolution**: Systems evolve toward configurations with higher Φ₄ (state trajectory variance from external influence), exploring new regions via Φ₅ (phase transitions).

**Cross-reference**: DE_RERUM_TODO_EXISTENS_COMPLETE.md, Chapter Addendum: The 28-Dimensional State Manifold

### Spation
The incompressible, Planck-scale particles that fill all space in a dodecahedral packing structure. The nearest-neighbor spacing between spations is ℓ_P ≈ 1.616×10⁻³⁵ m (Planck length). All matter is displacement of the spation medium.

**Cross-reference:** Phase 20, Section 20.1.1

### Displacement
Regions where spations cannot exist, creating "holes" in the spation field. Matter is fundamentally displacement, not mass. Particles are stable vortex structures that displace the spation medium.

**Cross-reference:** Atomica Sentis, Section 2

### Shunt Dynamics
The fundamental process of discrete displacement of matter through the spation medium via contact-only propagation. Energy is kinetic movement; there is no potential energy in SDT.

### Universal Background Pressure (P₀)
The fundamental pressure of the spation medium, P₀ ≈ 8.0×10³⁴ Pa. This pressure is the origin of all confinement forces and particle stability.

**Cross-reference:** Atomica Sentis, Section 2.1

## Mathematical Symbols & Parameters

### α (Alpha) - Fine Structure Constant
The ratio of electron orbital velocity to light speed: α = v_e/c ≈ 1/137.036. In SDT, this is not a coupling constant but a kinematic ratio emerging from spation mechanics.

**Physical meaning:** Represents the scale separation between spation deformation (Compton wavelength) and orbital scale (Bohr radius).

**Cross-reference:** Atomica Sentis, Section 4.1

### β (Beta) - Gravitational Compactness Parameter
Stellar/planetary compactness parameter: β = c²R_c/k². Governs orbital dynamics at all scales from atomic to galactic.

**Cross-reference:** Phase 20, Section 20.3

### Ϟ (Kappa Factor) - Velocity Ratio
The universal velocity ratio: Ϟ = c/v_surface, where v_surface is orbital velocity at reference radius R. Measures how "slow" orbits are compared to light speed.

**Physical meaning:** Lower Ϟ (faster orbits) → electrons; Higher Ϟ (slower orbits) → planets.

**Cross-reference:** Phase 20

### ϟ (Koppa) - c-Boundary Position
Geometric locus where local tangential speed reaches c. Related to Ϟ by: ϟ = R/Ϟ². Marks the transition from sub-luminal to relativistic regimes.

### κ (Kappa) - Geometric Efficiency
Geometric efficiency parameter in pressure calculations, typically κ ≈ 1. Accounts for non-ideal geometric contributions to force.

### ξ (Xi) - Screening Factor
Occlusion screening factor: ξ = N_eff/N_total. Explains the weakness of gravity (~10⁻⁹ relative to Coulomb) via mutual shadowing of pressure gradients.

**Cross-reference:** Phase 20, Phase 21

### k-factor (k) - Orbital Parameter
Universal orbital parameter governing velocity: v(r) = (c/k)√(R_c/r). Ranges from k_atom ≈ 137 (electrons) to k_stellar ≈ 10⁵ (planets).

**Derivation:** For atoms, k = 1/(α√Z). For stellar systems, determined from orbital data.

**Cross-reference:** Phase 20, Atomica Sentis Section 0.8

### z·k² = 1 Relation
Universal relationship for continuous mass distributions where z = 2R_c/D (compactness ratio). This does NOT hold for atomic systems (quantized).

**Cross-reference:** Phase 20, Phase 22

## Pressure & Force Concepts

### Occlusion Function E(x,n̂)
Directional occlusion function representing mutual shadowing between particles. Determines how much pressure is blocked in direction n̂ at position x.

**Master Equation:** ∇·[K_bulk ∇Δ] = -κ ρ_disp(1-E)

**Physical meaning:** E→0 (no screening) gives Coulomb force. E→1-δ (near-total screening) gives gravity.

**Cross-reference:** Phase 20, Atomica Sentis Section 0.7

### Displacement Density (ρ_disp)
Volume density of displaced spation: ρ_disp = V_displaced/V_total. Represents source strength for pressure gradients.

### K_bulk - Spation Bulk Modulus
Elastic stiffness of the spation lattice: K_bulk ≈ 4.6×10¹¹³ Pa. Determines compressibility and wave speed via c² = K_bulk/ρ_s.

**Cross-reference:** Phase 20, Section 20.1.2

### Geometric Charge (S)
Total interaction strength of a source: S = R_eff/k². Empirically determined from orbital data via S = v²r/c².

**Units:** Meters [m]

## Atomic Structure

### Resonant Cavity
Stable, nested spherical low-pressure zones created by the nucleus. Electron vortices occupy these cavities to achieve equilibrium. Shells n=1, 2, 3... correspond to nested cavities.

**Cross-reference:** Atomica Sentis, Section 10.1

### Helical Wake
The pressure disturbance created by a spinning vortex (electron or proton). Helical wakes from multiple vortices can constructively or destructively interfere, determining stability.

**Physical consequence:** Determines Pauli exclusion (opposite spins allow wake cancellation).

### Pauli Exclusion (SDT Interpretation)
Two vortices can occupy the same cavity only if they have opposite chirality (spin). Same-spin vortices create destructive wake interference.

**Cross-reference:** Atomica Sentis, Section 10.1, Rule 3

### Z_eff - Effective Nuclear Charge
Reduced nuclear charge experienced by outer electrons due to inner electron screening: Z_eff = Z(1 - ⟨E_inner⟩).

**Mechanism:** Inner electrons create pressure shadows, reducing gradient experienced by outer electrons.

**Cross-reference:** Atomica Sentis, Section 6.1

### Noble Gas Configuration
Geometrically serene electron arrangements (dyad for He, cube for Ne, etc.) with zero net geometric stress. These configurations are chemically inert.

**Physical meaning:** All helical wakes cancel, displacement field is symmetric.

## Galactic & Cosmological

### Eclipse Function E(r)
Cumulative occlusion by galactic disk mass interior to radius r. At large r, E→saturation, causing flat rotation curves.

**Cross-reference:** Phase 24, Phase 25

### Disk Eclipse Saturation
Mechanism explaining flat galactic rotation without dark matter. Beyond r ≈ R_d, additional disk mass adds negligible shadowing (saturation), causing v(r) ≈ constant.

**Cross-reference:** Phase 24

### R_flat - Flat Rotation Onset Radius
Galactocentric radius where rotation curve transitions from Keplerian (v ∝ 1/√r) to flat (v ≈ constant). Predicted: R_flat ≈ 2.5 R_d.

### Darkstar
SDT term for supermassive black hole. Not a singularity but an ultra-dense toroidal vortex structure at galactic core.

## Nuclear Structure

### Toroidal Vortex
Fundamental structure of nucleons (protons, neutrons). Stable, ring-shaped displacement structures held together by pressure gradients.

**Dimensions:** R_p ≈ 0.84 fm (proton), R_n ≈ 0.8 fm (neutron)

**Cross-reference:** Phase 17

### Baryonic Confinement
Self-confinement force holding protons together: F_confine ≈ 1.77×10⁵ N. This is the mechanical origin of the Strong Nuclear Force.

**Derivation:** F = P₀ · A_p where A_p = πR_p²

**Cross-reference:** Atomica Sentis, Section 2.2

### Leptonic Confinement
Self-confinement force holding electrons together: F_confine ≈ 2.00×10⁶ N. Novel SDT prediction (no Standard Model analog).

**Cross-reference:** Atomica Sentis, Section 3.1
