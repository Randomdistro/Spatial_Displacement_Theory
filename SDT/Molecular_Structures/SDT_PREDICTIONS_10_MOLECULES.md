## BASELINE NUCLEAR STRUCTURES (Reference)

**Carbon (C-12):**
- Nuclear Structure: 3 alpha particles (triangular arrangement)
- Composition: 6 protons + 6 neutrons = 12 nucleons
- Nuclear Field Strength: 12x
- Building Blocks: 3α
- First Ionization Energy: 11.260 eV

**Nitrogen (N-14):**
- Nuclear Structure: "Locked Gear" - Carbon-12 core (3α) + deuteron (p-n pair)
- Composition: 7 protons + 7 neutrons = 14 nucleons
- Nuclear Field Strength: 14x
- Building Blocks: 3α + [p,n]
- First Ionization Energy: 14.534 eV

**Oxygen (O-16):**
- Nuclear Structure: "Double Magic Tetrahedron" - 4 alpha particles (tetrahedral)
- Composition: 8 protons + 8 neutrons = 16 nucleons
- Nuclear Field Strength: 16x
- Building Blocks: 4α
- First Ionization Energy: 13.618 eV

**Hydrogen (H-1):**
- Nuclear Structure: Single proton
- Composition: 1 proton = 1 nucleon
- Nuclear Field Strength: 1x (baseline)
- First Ionization Energy: 13.598 eV
