## PART I: VOLUME SCOPE AND ORGANIZATION

### 1.1 Volume Theme

Volume 8 focuses on **the halogen family** - fluorine, chlorine, bromine, iodine, and astatine, along with their compounds. These elements share a unique geometric tragedy: **The Cube Minus One** - seven valence electrons occupying seven of eight vertices of a perfect cube, leaving a single geometric vacancy that drives their extreme reactivity. This volume covers hydrogen halides, diatomic halogens, interhalogens, halide salts, halogen oxides, organohalides, and polyhalides.

**Key Characteristics:**
- High electronegativity (nuclear field strength creates geometric vacuum)
- Strong oxidizing agents (driven by geometric hunger to complete cube)
- Form stable anions (halide ions complete the cube)
- Diatomic molecules (X₂) with varying stability
- Wide range of oxidation states
- Form strong bonds with most elements

### 1.2 Classification Criteria

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Molecules containing F, Cl, Br, I, At
- Hydrogen halides (HX)
- Diatomic halogens (X₂)
- Interhalogen compounds (XY, XY₃, XY₅, XY₇)
- Halide salts (metal halides)
- Halogen oxides and oxoacids
- Organohalides (organic compounds with halogens)
- Polyhalides and halogen complexes
- Halogen-containing ions

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Simple halides already in Volume 1 (H₂, H₂O with halide ions)
- Complex organohalides better suited for organic volumes
- Highly unstable or theoretical compounds
