# Phase Chemistry: Organic Stereochemistry from Pressure Chirality

## Abstract

This phase derives stereochemistry (chirality, enantiomers, diastereomers, optical activity) from Spatial Displacement Theory (SDT) using asymmetric pressure fields. Chirality arises when pressure field geometry lacks mirror symmetry. Enantiomers have mirror-image pressure fields. Optical activity from pressure field rotation. All from pressure field mechanics.

## 1. Physical Foundation

### 1.1 Master Equation for Stereochemistry

Stereochemistry derives from pressure field geometry:

$$\boxed{\dot{E} = P_{\mathrm{CMB}} \cdot A_{\mathrm{eff}} \cdot \Gamma \cdot \kappa \cdot (1-\eta)} \tag{1.1}$$

For chiral molecules:
- $A_{\mathrm{eff}}$ = asymmetric occlusion area
- $\Gamma$ = circulation factor (depends on geometry)
- $\kappa$ = curvature (molecular shape)
- $(1-\eta)$ = coupling strength (different for enantiomers)

**Chiral energy difference:**
Enantiomers have identical pressure field energy but opposite geometry.

### 1.2 Chirality from Asymmetric Pressure Fields

Chiral molecule: Cannot be superimposed on mirror image

**Requirement:** No plane of symmetry in pressure field geometry

**From master equation:**
Chirality requires:
$$A_{\mathrm{eff,L}} \neq A_{\mathrm{eff,R}} \quad \text{or} \quad \Gamma_L \neq \Gamma_R \tag{1.2}$$

where L, R refer to left- and right-handed forms.

**Example:** Lactic acid has chiral center (C with 4 different groups)

## 2. Enantiomers

### 2.1 Mirror-Image Pressure Fields

Enantiomers: Mirror-image molecules
- Same physical properties (except optical activity)
- Different biological activity
- Rotate plane-polarized light in opposite directions

**SDT explanation:** Mirror-image pressure field geometries.

---

## 3. Diastereomers

### 3.1 Non-Mirror Stereoisomers

Diastereomers: Stereoisomers that are not mirror images
- Different physical properties
- Example: cis/trans isomers

## 4. Optical Activity

### 4.1 Rotation of Plane-Polarized Light

Chiral molecules rotate plane of polarization:
- Dextrorotatory (+): Clockwise
- Levorotatory (-): Counterclockwise

**SDT explanation:** Asymmetric pressure field interacts differently with left/right circularly polarized light.
