## 1. Introduction: The Neutron Anomaly
Standard Physics treats the Neutron as a fundamental particle, distinct from the Proton and Electron, composed of quarks (udd). However, Beta Decay ($n \to p + e + \bar{\nu}_e$) suggests a deeper connection: the Neutron *contains* the components of a Proton and an Electron.
SDT proposes that the Neutron is not a fundamental particle, but a **Geometric Composite State**: a Proton with an Electron captured in a "Superluminal" or "Overtightened" orbital phase at the nuclear surface.

## 2. The Nestled State (The Central Well)
Instead of orbiting on the surface, the Electron is captured **Inside the Proton Torus**.
*   **Location:** The central "Donut Hole" of the Proton's Shuriken geometry.
*   **Dynamics:** The Electron sits in the magnetic/pressure potential well ("The Valley") created by the Proton's poloidal flow.
*   **Velocity:** Shielded by the Proton's flow, the electron moves significantly slower than the 1.84c rim velocity. It is "snug."

### 2.1 Stability
This "Nestled" state is stable because the electron is trapped behind the **Energy Barrier** of the Proton's high-speed rim. To escape, it must climb out of the well.

## 3. Beta Decay: The Escape
Beta decay is the **Ejection** of this nestled electron.
1.  **Trigger:** A perturbation destabilizes the well.
2.  **Climb:** The electron is kicked out of the center and must cross the high-speed rim.
3.  **The Snap (Antineutrino):** As the electron rips free, it imparts a **Rotational Recoil** to the spation medium.
    *   **Conservation of Spin:** The electron takes "Left" spin; the medium recoils with "Right" spin.
    *   **The Antineutrino:** This "Reverse Twist" packet is the antineutrino. It is not a particle created from nothing; it is the geometric recoil of the break.

## 4. Conclusion
The Neutron is a **Proton Torus with a Nestled Electron**. It is a mechanical composite. Decay is the mechanical failure of the containment well.
