## Water Molecule: Complete Nuclear Analysis

### Nuclear Structure

**Oxygen Nucleus:**
- 4 alpha particles in tetrahedral arrangement
- 8 protons + 8 neutrons = 16 nucleons
- Creates strong nuclear gravitational/centripetal field

**Hydrogen Nuclei:**
- Single proton each
- Weak nuclear gravitational field
- Respond to oxygen's nuclear field

### Geometry Determination

**The 104.45° Angle:**
- Oxygen nucleus pulls both H nuclei toward it (nuclear attraction)
- Two H nuclei repel each other (nuclear-nuclear repulsion)
- Equilibrium angle minimizes total nuclear gravitational energy
- **NOT electron repulsion - nuclear forces**

### Bond Formation

**O-H "Bond":**
- Nuclear gravitational/centripetal force holds H nucleus in orbit around O nucleus
- Equilibrium distance (95.84 pm) where nuclear forces balance
- Electron orbits in the combined nuclear field
- **NOT electron sharing - nuclear force connection**

### Bond Energy

**467 kJ/mol = 4.84 eV:**
- Depth of nuclear gravitational well
- Energy required to overcome nuclear force
- **NOT electron-electron interaction energy**

## All Chemical Phenomena Explained by Nuclear Forces

### Bonding
- **Covalent:** Nuclear forces hold nuclei together
- **Ionic:** Strong nuclear field captures another nucleus
- **Metallic:** Nuclear fields overlap in extended structure

### Geometry
- **Linear:** Nuclear forces align nuclei in line
- **Bent:** Nuclear forces balance at angle
- **Tetrahedral:** Nuclear field creates tetrahedral geometry

### Reactivity
- **Electronegativity:** Nuclear field strength (more nucleons = stronger field)
- **Ionization:** Overcoming nuclear gravitational well
- **Redox:** Nuclear field rearrangements

### Spectroscopy
- **Electronic transitions:** Electron moves between nuclear field energy levels
- **Vibrational modes:** Nuclei vibrate in nuclear force field
- **Rotational modes:** Molecule rotates while nuclear forces maintain geometry
