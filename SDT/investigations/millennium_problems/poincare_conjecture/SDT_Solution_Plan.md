**Note:** This was proven by Perelman (2003), but the SDT approach provides a physical interpretation.

**Core Insight:** Topological manifolds are not abstract mathematical spaces but **physical spation configurations**. The Poincaré Conjecture asks whether a simply connected spation region (no "holes") is topologically equivalent to a sphere - the simplest possible spation configuration.

- **Homeomorphic to 3-Sphere** → Can be continuously deformed to uniform spation (no displacement)
