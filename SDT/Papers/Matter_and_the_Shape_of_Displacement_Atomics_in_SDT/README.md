## Purpose

This library provides **rigorously derived, SDT-based documentation** for every element of the periodic table. Unlike standard chemistry references that rely on quantum mechanical probability interpretations, these documents present **deterministic geometric models** of atomic structure.

**Each element receives 5 comprehensive files**:

1. **On [Element]**: Brief overview and position in SDT framework
2. **Nuclear Structure**: Geometry, neutrino flux, binding energy
3. **Valence Shell**: Electron positions, bonding character, orbitals as geometric paths
4. **Ions and Isotopes**: Stability analysis, nuclear variations
5. **Excitations**: Spectral lines, energy levels as harmonic modes

**Example**: Carbon's sp² geometry (120° bonds) arises from triangular arrangement of 3 alpha clusters, NOT from hybridization math.
