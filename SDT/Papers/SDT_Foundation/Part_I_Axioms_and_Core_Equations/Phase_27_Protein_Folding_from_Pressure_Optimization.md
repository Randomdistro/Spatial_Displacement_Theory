# Phase 27: Protein Folding from Pressure Optimization

## Abstract

This phase explains protein folding from Spatial Displacement Theory (SDT) using pressure field minimization. The native protein structure minimizes total occlusion pressure energy. Folding pathways follow pressure gradient descent. Secondary structure (α-helices, β-sheets) emerges from local pressure field optimization. The theory addresses the Levinthal paradox by providing a deterministic folding mechanism.

---

## 1. Protein Structure as Pressure Minimum

### 1.1 Native State

The native (folded) protein structure minimizes:
$$U_{\text{total}} = \sum_{\text{residues}} U_{\text{occlusion}} + \sum_{\text{interactions}} U_{\text{interaction}} \tag{1.1}$$

### 1.2 Folding Pathway

Folding follows pressure gradient:
$$\frac{d\vec{r}_i}{dt} = -\gamma \nabla_i U_{\text{total}} \tag{1.2}$$

### 1.3 Secondary Structure

**α-helices:** Optimal occlusion geometry for polypeptide chains
**β-sheets:** Extended occlusion patterns maximizing inter-strand interactions
