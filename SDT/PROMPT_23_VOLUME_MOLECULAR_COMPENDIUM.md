   - Comprehend the Associated Data Ledger structure
   - Know the nuclear structure from `atomica_sentis_nuclei_per_nucei.md` for every element involved

2. **Understand the Movement Budget Model**:
   - Each atom has three toroidal degrees of freedom (rotational axes)
   - When one degree of freedom is constrained by bonding, it affects the other two
   - This constraint manifests as:
     - **Interrotation delay**: The constrained rotation transfers energy to the other two axes
     - **Overpowering**: If the constraint is too severe, the system cannot resolve the energy imbalance and the bond breaks or the molecule becomes unstable
   - The movement budget is quantified as: `E_budget = E_rotational + E_vibrational + E_translational`
   - Bond formation reduces available rotational energy, increasing vibrational modes

3. **Master SDT Bonding Mechanics**:
   - Covalent bonds: Shared electron vortices creating pressure field overlap
   - Ionic bonds: Complete electron transfer creating charge separation
   - Metallic bonds: Delocalized electron sea
   - Hydrogen bonds: Weak dipole-dipole interactions
   - Van der Waals: Residual pressure field interactions
   - All bonds are pressure field equilibria, not force-based

4. **Understand the Structural Event Chain**:
   - **Precursive State**: Molecular geometry before interaction
   - **Excitation Requirements**: Energy needed to break bonds or promote electrons
   - **Ionization Thresholds**: Energy to remove electrons completely
   - **Bond Formation**: Pressure field equilibration process
   - **Stabilization**: Final geometric configuration
   - **Reactivity Profile**: How the molecule interacts with others

### 2.4 Movement Budget Analysis

**A. Toroidal Freedom Assessment**:

For EACH atom, calculate:

1. **Pre-Bonding State**:
   - Rotational freedom: `E_rot = (1/2)Iω²` for each of three axes
   - Available movement budget: `E_budget,free`

2. **Post-Bonding State**:
   - Constrained axes: Which rotations are restricted by bonds
   - Remaining rotational energy: `E_rot,constrained`
   - Vibrational modes created: `E_vib = (n-1) × (1/2)hν` where n = number of bonds
   - Translational energy: `E_trans = (1/2)mv²`

3. **Energy Redistribution**:
   - Energy transferred from rotation to vibration: `ΔE = E_rot,free - E_rot,constrained`
   - Interrotation delay: Time constant for energy transfer between axes
   - Overpowering threshold: Maximum constraint before bond failure

**B. Quantification**:
- Calculate exact energy values in J, eV, and kJ/mol
- Determine if system is stable (energy can be redistributed) or unstable (overpowering)
- Identify which bonds are "stressed" (high constraint) vs "relaxed" (low constraint)
