## The "Fourth Wall" Concept

### Theater Analogy

**In theater:**
- The "fourth wall" is the invisible boundary between the stage and the audience
- The audience watches through this invisible wall
- It separates the observer from what's being observed

### In Nuclear Packing Geometry

**The "fourth wall" is:**
- **The audience** - the sixth sphere
- Sits **between us** (the observers contemplating the structure) **and the structure** (the dodecahedron)
- **Blocks us** from the stage (the dodecahedron)
- It's the **boundary** that separates observer from observed
