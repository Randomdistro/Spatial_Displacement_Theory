## 2. Conformational Analysis

### 2.1 Ethane Conformations

**Staggered:** Lower energy (preferred)

**Eclipsed:** Higher energy

## 3. Cycloalkanes

### 3.1 Ring Strain

Cycloalkanes have angle strain from non-ideal bond angles:

**Cyclopropane:**
- 60° angles (vs. ideal 109.5°)
- High strain energy: ~115 kJ/mol

**Cyclobutane:**
- 90° angles
- Strain energy: ~110 kJ/mol

**Cyclohexane:**
- Chair conformation: 109.5° angles
- Minimal strain: ~0 kJ/mol

**SDT explanation:** Strain = pressure field energy from non-optimal geometry.
