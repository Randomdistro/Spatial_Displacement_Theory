## 1. Physical Foundation

### 1.1 Master Equation for Electrochemistry

All electrochemical processes derive from the master equation:

$$\boxed{\dot{E} = P_{\mathrm{CMB}} \cdot A_{\mathrm{eff}} \cdot \Gamma \cdot \kappa \cdot (1-\eta)} \tag{1.1}$$

For electrochemical cells:
- $A_{\mathrm{eff}}$ = effective electron transfer area
- $\Gamma$ = electron circulation factor
- $\kappa$ = curvature (inverse electrode distance)
- $(1-\eta)$ = electron pressure field coupling strength

**Cell potential:**
$$E_{\mathrm{cell}} = \frac{\Delta \dot{E}_{\mathrm{electron}}}{nF} \times \tau_{\mathrm{transfer}} \tag{1.2}$$

where $n$ = number of electrons, $F$ = Faraday constant.
