## Key Observations

### Energy Scaling

**Total energy scales approximately as Z²:**
- H (Z=1): -13.6 eV
- He (Z=2): -108.8 eV (8×)
- Ne (Z=10): -3700.8 eV (272×)
- Ar (Z=18): -14187.0 eV (1043×)
- Kr (Z=36): -71012.6 eV (5221×)
- Cd (Z=48): -145344.5 eV (10687×)

**Pattern:** E_total ≈ -13.6 × Z² × f_screening

### Compactness

**Compactness decreases with Z:**
- H, He: 1.000 (single shell)
- Li-Be: 0.08-0.03 (2 shells)
- B-Ne: 0.02-0.07 (2-3 shells)
- Higher Z: 0.03-0.13 (multiple shells)

**Interpretation:**
- Compactness = min_r / max_r
- Measures spread of electron distribution
- Lower = more spread out (multiple shells)

### Geometric Arrangements

**Alpha clusters:**
- All elements have at least 1 alpha cluster
- Multiple alphas for heavier elements
- Poles defined by alpha structure

**Electron shells:**
- 1s: Paired opposites
- 2s: Concentric ring
- 2p: Tetrahedral unpaired
- Higher shells: Concentric rings with neighbor-dependent rotation
