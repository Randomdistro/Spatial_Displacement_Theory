## First Shell Structure

### What I Understand:

**Central sphere:**
- Radius: $r$
- At origin: $(0, 0, 0)$

**12 outer spheres:**
- Each has radius: $r$
- Centers are at distance: $2r$ from origin
- Each outer sphere center is exactly $2r$ from every other outer sphere center
- Arranged in icosahedral pattern (12 vertices of icosahedron)

**Visualization:**
```
        Outer sphere (radius r)
        Center at distance 2r from origin
        ──────────────────────────────
        
        Central sphere (radius r)
        At origin
        
        ──────────────────────────────
        Outer sphere (radius r)
        Center at distance 2r from origin
```

**Total width:** $6r$ (central sphere extends from $-r$ to $+r$, outer spheres extend from $r$ to $3r$, so total $6r$)

**The 12 dots:**
- 12 points on surface of sphere at radius $2r$
- Each point is $2r$ from its neighbors
- Arranged in triangular fashion (icosahedral vertices)

**Octahedral spaces:**
- 2 zones where 2 adjacent dots are slightly more than $2r$ apart
- These zones are on opposite sides of the $2r$ sphere
- Each zone forms an octahedral space with:
  - 5 surrounding positions (from 5 nearest outer spheres)
  - 1 reference position (the "fourth wall")
  - Total: 6 positions per octahedral space

## Second Layer Structure

### What I Understand:

**Total width:** $10r$ (from center to outer edge)

**Positions:**
- Located in **triangular interstices** between first-shell spheres
- An icosahedron has 20 triangular faces
- Each triangular face = 3 first-shell spheres forming a triangle
- Each triangle has 1 triangular interstice (the gap in the middle)
- **Total: 20 triangular interstices**

**Critical property:**
- These second-layer positions **do not touch each other**
- They are isolated positions in the triangular gaps
- Each is surrounded by 3 first-shell spheres (forming the triangle)

**Visualization:**
```
First shell: 12 spheres at radius 2r
            (forming icosahedron)

Second layer: 20 positions in triangular interstices
              (in the gaps between first-shell spheres)
              These don't touch each other
```
